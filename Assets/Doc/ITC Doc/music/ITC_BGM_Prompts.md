# ITC《地狱转运公司》背景音乐生成提示词 (Prompts)

## 1. BGM_Hell_Hotel (地狱旅馆 - 苏醒)

**Prompt:**
Instrumental underscore ONLY. Seamless loop. Background music for an opening scene (non-feature, minimal).
60–80 BPM, 4/4, 45–70 seconds, clean loop (no big ending, no sudden risers).
**Scene:** Waking up in a shabby, dusty 19th-century hotel room in Hell. Confusion, memory loss, static silence, ancient decay.
**Mood keywords:** eerie, disorienting, dusty, hollow, melancholic, sparse, unsettling silence.
**Harmony:** Atonal or ambiguous minor key (C minor or G minor), dissonant intervals, lack of resolution.
**Instrumentation (strictly non-vocal):**
*   Detuned Upright Piano (playing slow, isolated single notes, high reverb)
*   Subtle Wind/Air texture (hissing, room tone)
*   Glass or Metal friction sounds (very quiet, textural)
*   Vibraphone (bowed or struck softly, creating a ghostly resonance)
**Composition:** Extremely sparse arrangement. Focus on texture over melody. Long pauses between piano notes. No rhythmic drive.
**NO (negative constraints):**
No vocals. No singing. No choir. No lyrics.
No drums. No percussion beats. No rhythmic pulse.
No happy major chords. No heroic themes. No orchestral swells. No fast tempo. No electronic synths.

## 2. BGM_Skeleton_Encounter (骷髅门童 - 黑色滑稽)

**Prompt:**
Instrumental underscore ONLY. Seamless loop. Background music for character dialogue.
90–110 BPM, 2/4 or 4/4, 40–60 seconds, clean loop.
**Scene:** Meeting a talking skeleton bellboy in a hotel hallway. Dark comedy, dry humor, awkward silence broken by bone rattles.
**Mood keywords:** quirky, dry, deadpan, spooky-funny, rhythmic, skeletal.
**Harmony:** Harmonic Minor scale (D harmonic minor), staccato articulation, mischievous tone.
**Instrumentation (strictly non-vocal):**
*   Xylophone or Marimba (prominent, mimicking bone sounds)
*   Pizzicato Double Bass (walking bass line, clumsy)
*   Bassoon or Bass Clarinet (playing staccato low notes)
*   Woodblocks (percussive clicking)
**Composition:** "plodding" rhythm, sparse but rhythmic. A "call and response" feel between the woodwinds and the xylophone. Playful but dead.
**NO (negative constraints):**
No vocals. No singing. No choir. No lyrics.
No epic orchestral strings. No electric guitars. No heavy distortion.
No sad or emotional melodies. No high-energy dance beats.

## 3. BGM_Saint_NY_City (圣纽约市 - 工业奇观)

**Prompt:**
Instrumental underscore ONLY. Seamless loop. Background music for dialogue (non-feature, non-song).
112–124 BPM, 4/4, 45–70 seconds, clean loop (no big ending, no one-way risers).
**Scene:** arrival at “Saint New York City”, a booming early-1900s industrial frontier metropolis—overwhelming scale, machinery, ambition, and absurd capitalist spectacle; the feeling is cold, ironic, unsettlingly funny.
**Mood keywords:** dark comedy, deadpan irony, cold neon (not sunny), uncanny bustle, cynical wonder; tension under a smile.
**Harmony:** favor minor / modal (Dorian/Phrygian hints), chromatic passing tones, tritone touches, occasional wrong-note humor; avoid bright major resolution.
**Instrumentation (strictly non-vocal):**
*   Upright piano in low-mid register playing a stiff, slightly mechanical ostinato (hint of ragtime syncopation but degraded/awkward, not cheerful)
*   Muted brass used as short, sarcastic stabs (no warm swells, no heroic chords)
*   Bass clarinet / bari sax as cold pads (no solos)
*   Pizzicato strings as “nervous ticks”
*   Dry snare/brushes + small metallic clinks like machinery rhythm (kept subtle)
*   Quiet industrial rumble texture (steam hiss very low)
**Composition:** repetitive 16-bar loop with call-and-response between piano ostinato and muted brass stabs but keep it dry and deadpan; narrow melodic range; steady dynamics; no uplifting lift; mix mid-focused, short room reverb, slightly gritty/tape-worn feel.
**NO (negative constraints):**
No vocals. No singing. No choir. No humming. No scat. No spoken words. No whispers. No “ah/oh” vocal pads. No vocal chops. No lyrics.
No upbeat happy major-key pop feel. No heroic anthem. No warm cinematic swell. No big-band celebration.
No catchy singable topline hook. No bright EDM synths. Avoid shiny hi-hats and bright sparkling bells.

## 4. BGM_ITC_Elevator (公司电梯 - 诡异Muzak)

**Prompt:**
Instrumental underscore ONLY. Seamless loop. Background music for a specific environment (Elevator).
100–120 BPM, 3/4 (Waltz) or 4/4 (Bossa Nova), 30–50 seconds, clean loop.
**Scene:** Inside a corporate elevator in Hell. Cheesy, low-fidelity background music trying to be pleasant but feeling fake and brainwashing.
**Mood keywords:** kitsch, fake-happy, lo-fi, warped, plastic, unsettlingly cheerful.
**Harmony:** Simple Major chords (C Major) but slightly detuned or pitch-wobbling.
**Instrumentation (strictly non-vocal):**
*   Hammond Organ or Cheesy Synth Organ (melody)
*   Muted Guitar (playing simple rhythm)
*   Drum Machine (very stiff, low-fi bossa nova beat)
*   Tape Hiss / Vinyl Crackle (audio texture)
**Composition:** Repetitive, simplistic "holding music" melody. Pitch wavers slightly (wow and flutter effects) to suggest a broken tape.
**NO (negative constraints):**
No vocals. No real lyrics (unless garbled beyond recognition).
No high-fidelity production. No dynamic changes. No dramatic buildup.
No complex jazz improvisation. No orchestral instruments.

## 7. BGM_Gameplay_Main (签约主循环 - 灵魂流水线)

**Prompt:**
Instrumental underscore ONLY. Seamless loop. Background music for main gameplay loop (puzzle/paperwork).
80–100 BPM, 4/4, 60–90 seconds, clean loop.
**Scene:** Sitting at a desk, processing documents for souls. monotonous but tense. A mix of boredom and the pressure of making mistakes.
**Mood keywords:** focused, mechanical, repetitive, subtly tense, bureaucratic, thinking music.
**Harmony:** Modal (Dorian), persistent bass line, minimal melodic movement.
**Instrumentation (strictly non-vocal):**
*   Double Bass (repetitive ostinato line)
*   Piano (sparse, low-register chords)
*   Light Percussion (paper shuffling sounds, light tapping, brush snare)
*   Muted Trombone (occasional low swells)
**Composition:** Minimalist. A steady, unyielding rhythm that helps the player focus but feels slightly oppressive. Not distracting.
**NO (negative constraints):**
No vocals. No singing.
No catchy melodies that get annoying on loop. No high-pitched lead instruments.
No sudden loud crashes. No silence. No dramatic changes in tempo.
